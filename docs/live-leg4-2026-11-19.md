## Open Finding

### Low: Permanent Structural Damage Never Enters Contingency Mode Through The UI

The live structural QRH stayed headed **EMERGENCY QRH: STRUCTURAL AIRFRAME DAMAGE**, despite its permanent classification. REC was disabled and ATTEMPT RECOVERY was absent. There was therefore no normal UI action to produce the guide's required **CONTINGENCY SOLUTION** transition for this permanent failure.

The existing procedure already gives permanent-damage instructions: reduce below 220 kt, avoid greater than 1.5 G maneuvers, and divert. This is a presentation/workflow mismatch rather than evidence of missing damage guidance or an ineffective failure.

Source inspection agrees with the observation: `emitFailure` sends `recoverable: false`, but the checklist's `failure:triggered` handler initializes `contingency: false` for every failure. A contingency event is sent only after a failed recovery attempt, which structural damage's UI prevents. Either route permanent failures directly to contingency mode or reconcile the live verification guide with the intended presentation.
