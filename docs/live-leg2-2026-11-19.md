## Confirmed Finding

### Medium: Lights Checklist Reports Completion Without Actuation

Clicking the Phenom's `Landing & Strobe Lights - ON` row changed its checkbox to complete. The implementation in `src/modules/checklist-system/index.ts` calls optional `controls.setters.setLights.set`, then returns success and records manual completion. In the live V4 session, `controls.setters.setLights?.set` was undefined. Consequently that click invoked no simulator lights setter. Telemetry continued to report `lightsOn: false`.

The missing setter establishes an action failure independently of the suspended telemetry. Verify the correct native light controls and ensure hardware rows cannot indicate verified completion solely from a click. This run made no source fixes.
