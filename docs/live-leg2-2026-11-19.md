# Leg 2 Live Verification — November 19, 2026

> [!CAUTION]
> **Educational And Fair-Play Disclaimer**: Use this extension for education and single-player simulation. Respect GeoFS subscriptions, ad-reward entitlements, multiplayer rules, and access controls.

## Verdict

**Leg 2 fails functional verification on GeoFS V4.** Live checks confirmed missing light actuation, fuel weight that does not update rigid-body mass, and fuel burn that ignores actual engine thrust on the Phenom 100. Two short takeoff rolls were completed after the user kept GeoFS active. These results supersede the earlier paused-test status in this report.

No extension source fixes were made. V3, deliberate rotation/pitch-response comparison and expanded GUIDE content remain untested.

## Environment And Method

- Source and unpacked `dist/` synchronized to remote `main` commit `ad60f48`; extension v0.2.1. This report was initially pushed in `5bfe646` and subsequently extended with resumed live testing.
- User confirmed extension and simulator reload, then confirmed GeoFS was active for resumed testing.
- GeoFS V4, Embraer Phenom 100, aircraft ID `5`, existing user browser profile. This was not an extension-only profile.
- Initial airborne aircraft was moved using native `geofs.flyTo`. For measured rolls, used the simulator's KSFO runway threshold `[37.60636, -122.38103]`, heading 27.73 degrees, calm wind, flap notch 1 (10 degrees), idle engines near 1,000 RPM, both throttles commanded to full, and neutral elevator/aileron.
- Initial simulation speed was 4. An exploratory roll at that setting was excluded. Both reported comparison rolls used speed 1. Sampling ran approximately once per second using current telemetry; times below are wall-clock values, affected by browser scheduling and frame rate.
- Each comparison roll ended after roughly 12 seconds, followed by idle throttle and brakes. Neither was a full takeoff or deliberate rotation test.

## Confirmed Findings

### High: Fuel Weight Changes Do Not Reach The Rigid Body

With AUTO-REFUEL disabled, the slider changed displayed gross weight and `definition.mass` from about 4,744 kg at 100% fuel to 3,599 kg at 10%. The live rigid-body `mass` stayed 4,200 kg and `s_inverseMass` stayed approximately 0.000238095 at both settings. A temporary observer around the actual body's `setMassProps` recorded zero calls.

`FuelSimulatorModule.syncMass` in `src/modules/fuel-simulator/index.ts` deliberately updates only `definition.mass`. `FUEL_MASS_SYNC` therefore does not establish a live rigid-body mass update. Fuel burn also reduces the definition mass while leaving body mass unchanged.

| Trial At Simulation Speed 1 | Initial Definition Mass | Body Mass Throughout | Interpolated Time To 50 KIAS | Final Sample |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 100% Fuel | 4,744.33 kg | 4,200 kg | 8.37 s | 73.84 KIAS at 12.28 s |
| 10% Fuel | 3,599.53 kg | 4,200 kg | 8.31 s | 75.72 KIAS at 12.70 s |

The runs show very similar acceleration despite approximately 1,145 kg difference in displayed weight. This is a short, single-pair comparison with scheduling variation, not a statistical proof of identical dynamics. The direct body-mass inspection independently confirms the mismatch. Neutral-control pitch stayed close to level during the rolls; no intentional pitch input was applied, so fuel-dependent rotation response remains unverified.

### Medium: Burn Uses Fixed Rated Thrust Instead Of Live Thrust

The Phenom's two engine objects each expose fixed `thrust: 7700` alongside variable `currentThrust`. The fuel calculation sums the fixed `thrust` fields. Observations with ALTITUDE BURN enabled near runway elevation:

- Idle throttles `[0, 0]`, both `currentThrust: 0`: **146 kg/h**.
- Full throttles `[1, 1]`, each live thrust near 7,700: **146 kg/h** throughout the roll.
- Asymmetric throttles `[0.5, 0]`, live thrust approximately `[3776, 0]`: **146 kg/h**.

The displayed burn does not follow actual thrust or the tested asymmetric throttle setting. Fuel continued draining at idle. ALTITUDE BURN and REAL TANKS were enabled; toggling ALTITUDE BURN at runway elevation did not change the rounded 146 kg/h readout, which alone cannot establish whether altitude scaling is correct. No high-altitude comparison was flown.

### Medium: Lights Checklist Reports Completion Without Actuation

Clicking `Landing & Strobe Lights - ON` checked the row, while `lightsOn` remained false in fresh, active telemetry. The implementation calls optional `controls.setters.setLights.set`; that function is absent in the live V4 session. It then accepts manual completion without actual light actuation.

The missing setter establishes an action failure independently of earlier suspended telemetry. Hardware rows should distinguish a clicked command from verified native state.

### Unattributed: Native Physics Froze While Extension Telemetry Continued

The first session stopped advancing native physics even after the tab became visible: `geofs.frameNumber` remained 1, native velocity was near zero, and animation ground speed retained about 199.8 kt from the initial airborne state. Extension timestamps advanced, so a recent telemetry timestamp did not guarantee fresh simulator animation values. Reloading restored native frame progression and plausible ground telemetry.

No error listener was present at original page startup; later listeners captured no uncaught errors. The root cause is unknown in this mixed browser profile. Earlier background-tab suspension was real but did not fully explain the visible-tab freeze. Do not attribute this to a specific module without reproduction and startup error capture.

## Tank Drain And Final State

With AUTO-REFUEL off and REAL TANKS on, set fuel to 1% (12.72 kg) for a short, parked drain observation. The initial distribution was center 5.088 kg, left/right 3.816 kg each. During center drain, a direct module-state inspection showed center 3.993 kg while both wings remained 3.816 kg. After roughly two minutes, center reached zero and both wings decreased symmetrically to approximately 3.542 kg each (7.085 kg total). This verifies the observed drain order and handoff, despite the separate burn-rate defect. No fuel-exhaustion test was attempted.

Restored fuel to 100%, AUTO-REFUEL / ALTITUDE BURN / REAL TANKS to their original enabled settings, flaps target to zero, throttles to idle, and the original simulation speed of 4. The aircraft is parked and paused at KSFO with parking brake set, ground contact true and crashed false. Ground speed immediately before pause was approximately 0.05 kt. The FUEL tab remains open for inspection.

Removed the temporary mass observer, stopped test sampling timers and removed the temporary module-event subscription. The mass observer recorded zero calls throughout the resumed tests. No extension code or unrelated local edits were changed.
